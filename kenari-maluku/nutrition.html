WHAT IS KENARI?

The Nut the World
Hasn't Discovered Yet.

Canarium indicum — also known as Kenari in Indonesia, Galip in Papua New Guinea, Ngali in Solomon Islands, and Nangai in Vanuatu — is a nut unlike any other. It belongs to the Burseraceae family, the same family as frankincense and myrrh trees.

Its fatty acid profile is remarkably similar to olive oil — dominated by heart-healthy oleic acid (~45–50%) — but with the added nutritional richness of a tree nut. Scientists have called it "one of the most nutritious nuts in the Asia-Pacific region."

Key Nutritional Highlights
Oleic Acid — Like Olive Oil

45–50% oleic acid — the same heart-healthy monounsaturated fat that makes extra-virgin olive oil famous. Stable, buttery, and deeply nourishing.

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Contains linolenic acid — essential Omega-3 — supporting cardiovascular health, brain function, and reducing systemic inflammation.

Vitamin E — Antioxidant Shield

High tocopherol (Vitamin E) content — a fat-soluble antioxidant protecting cells from oxidative stress. Critical for skin health and immunity.

Mineral Complex

Rich in Magnesium, Phosphorus, Iron, Zinc, Potassium, Selenium, Manganese, and Copper — essential for bone density, energy metabolism, and immune defense.

Phenolic Antioxidants

Contains phenolic compounds and tocopherols with high antioxidative activity — fighting free radicals and supporting long-term cellular health.

Scientific data from: Universitas Gadjah Mada, ACIAR Research, StuartXchange Phytochemical Studies, Nth Wonder Canarium Research.

HEALTH BENEFITS

What Kenari
Does For You

Backed by science. Proven by centuries of traditional use. The Kenari nut is more than food — it's medicine, energy, and vitality in every bite.

Heart Health

High oleic acid and Omega-3 content supports healthy cholesterol levels, reduces inflammation in arterial walls, and improves cardiovascular function — similar to a daily dose of olive oil.

*Supported by fatty acid profiling research — Canarium indicum
Brain Function

Omega-3 fatty acids are essential for cognitive function, memory, and mental clarity. Regular consumption supports brain health and may help reduce the risk of age-related cognitive decline.

*Linolenic acid content — polyunsaturated Omega-3 fat
Skin & Glow

Vitamin E and Kenari oil's emollient properties nourish skin from within. Traditionally used topically for eczema and psoriasis. Internally, it supports healthy, glowing skin through antioxidant action.

*Tocopherol + emollient oil profile studies
Bone & Muscle

Magnesium, Phosphorus, and Calcium content supports bone density and muscle function. The 14–16.5g protein per 100g makes it an excellent plant-based protein source for muscle maintenance.

*Mineral profile — Mg, P, Ca, Zn content analysis
Immunity & Anti-Inflammatory

Used in traditional medicine for rheumatism, angina, and respiratory issues. Modern research confirms anti-inflammatory phenolic compounds that support immune system resilience.

*Traditional medicine + phytochemical research
Sustained Energy

At 757 kcal per 100g with a healthy fat-to-carb ratio, Kenari provides slow-burning, long-lasting energy. An ideal meal-replacement or performance snack without the sugar crash.

*Energy density + low glycemic index profile
