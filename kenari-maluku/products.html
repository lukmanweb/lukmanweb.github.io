Roasted
Kenari

Our flagship product — wild Canarium kernels slow-roasted with fragrant Maluku garlic. The savory, umami-rich coating enhances the Kenari's natural buttery depth without overpowering its earthy authenticity.

Bold. Savory. Utterly addictive. Best enjoyed as a premium snack, on cheese boards, or crumbled over Indonesian dishes.

02 — Crunchy

ALGARI®

Crunchy Kenari Cluster

Clusters of whole Kenari kernels bound together with a whisper-light crisp coating. ALGARI delivers a unique textural experience — shatteringly crunchy outside, richly buttery inside — unlike any other nut product.

The name ALGARI derives from the Maluku word for the sound of cracking Kenari — a celebration of the nut's most satisfying characteristic.

Ingredients: Wild Kenari, Rice Flour, Natural Seasoning
Process: Clustered & oven-baked, no deep frying
Certified: Halal MUI · BPOM Registered
Export: HS Code 2008.19.91

03 — Sweet

NARIPONG®

Honey Roasted Kenari

Glazed with pure local wild honey — naturally sweet, deeply satisfying. NARIPONG marries the Kenari's rich forest earthiness with Maluku's wild floral honey in perfect harmony.

A timeless pairing of forest nut and forest honey. No refined sugar. No artificial flavors. Just two gifts from the same ancient forest.

Ingredients: Wild Kenari, Pure Wild Honey, Sea Salt

04 — Award Winning

GLOWRY®

Arengga Sugar Glazed Kenari

Our award-winning masterpiece — Kenari kernels glazed with traditional Arengga palm sugar (gula aren), creating a rich caramel-toffee depth that is uniquely and unmistakably Indonesian.

GLOWRY won the UKM Pangan Award for its innovation in combining Maluku's finest wild nut with one of Indonesia's most treasured traditional sweeteners. A product that tells two stories at once.
