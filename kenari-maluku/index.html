Canarium nuts (Canarium indicum) grow naturally in the forests of Maluku. We harvest them the traditional way — once a year, respecting nature's cycle. Only the best quality nuts are selected by local communities.

Every NATSEPA product is processed and packaged directly in Maluku, ensuring value is created at origin and empowering local communities who have nurtured these forests for generations.
